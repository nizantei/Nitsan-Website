# The 3D character: a low-poly you, built from one photo with free tools

Source: `Nitsan photo/Profile Picture.jpg`. Output: `work/models/nitsan.glb`, a stylized low-poly
character (~5k triangles, ~420 KB): flat-shaded facets, solid colors per region, slightly larger
head and hands, in the spirit of hand-made low-poly characters. Everything is scripted and
re-runnable; nothing is generated by a paid service, and everything runs on the CPU.

How it stays you: the facets of the face sit on your own face landmarks (fitted to the photo),
the regions (buzz-cut hairline, stubble, mustache, brows, eyes, the smile with teeth) are traced
from the photo, and the body keeps your build and the crossed-arms pose from the photo.

```bash
cd work/tools/model
./setup.sh             # once: Python venv (Blender 5.2 as a module, MediaPipe, numpy, scipy, pillow)
./build.sh             # photo → guide body → low-poly model → work/models/nitsan.glb (~1 minute)
./build.sh lowpoly     # only the low-poly step, after editing character.json → lowpoly (~15 s)
```

Previews: `build/preview/lowpoly_sheet.png` (turnaround and close-ups),
`build/preview/lowpoly_face_debug.png` (the face's triangles over the photo), and
`node stations.mjs` (three.js close-ups of every page stop; needs `npm run serve` in `work/tools`).
`build/lowpoly_stations.json` lists the stop heights (eyes, arms, belt, knees, ankles) as fractions
of the figure, for `stations.*.y` in `js/config.js`.

## Low-poly settings (`character.json` → `lowpoly`)

| Note | Setting |
|---|---|
| Bigger / smaller head or hands | `head_scale`, `hand_scale` |
| More / fewer facets | `skull_points` (head), `torso_sides`, `sleeve_sides`, `arm_sides`, `leg_sides` |
| Hair, mustache, beard, brows stand out more / less | `hair_raise`, `mustache_raise`, `beard_raise`, `brow_raise` |
| Eyes bigger / smaller, iris size | `eye_scale` (width, height), `iris_radius` |
| Any color (skin, hair, beard, tee, chinos, sneakers ...) | `palette` (sRGB hex) |
| Livelier / flatter facets | `jitter` (per-facet brightness variation) |
| Chain links, beads, sole height, sneaker size | `chain_link`, `bead`, `sole`, `shoe_extra` |

Shapes the regions follow: the hairline curve (`hairline_frame` in `lowpoly.py`), the stubble line
(sideburns to mouth corners), the mustache and mouth outlines (from the photo's landmarks).

## The guide (steps 1 to 4 below)

The low-poly model is measured off a guide: a realistic body fitted to the photo, with your
face shape, the crossed-arms pose and the clothes. `lowpoly.py` samples it with low-sided rings
(torso, arms, legs), places the face facets on its landmarks, and builds hands, sneakers, belt,
chain and bracelet as simple blocks around it. `./build.sh realistic` still builds the earlier
photoreal version (textures, enhanced photo, licensed sneaker model) into `build/`.

## How it works

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `analyze_photo.py` | MediaPipe (Apache-2.0) reads the photo: 478 face landmarks, body pose, and a hair / skin / clothes segmentation. |
| 1b | `enhance_photo.py` | A 4x sharper copy of the photo for close-ups: Real-ESRGAN x4 (BSD-3) upscale, GFPGAN v1.4 (Apache-2.0) face detail blended in at `texture.face_restore`, then the photo's own skin grain (freckles, stubble) put back at `texture.photo_grain`. |
| 2 | `face_corr.py` | Renders the generic MakeHuman head in Blender, runs the same landmarker on it, and ray-casts each landmark onto the mesh, so the photo and the 3D head share landmarks. |
| 3 | `fit_face.py` | Fits MakeHuman's face-shape targets and expression units (smile, squint, ...) to your landmarks, then warps away most of the leftover error. |
| 4 | `build_character.py` (+ `garments.py`, `shoes.py`, `extras.py`, `bake.py`) | Body from MakeHuman macros, skeleton, crossed arms by IK aimed at the photo. Tee and pleated chinos cut from the body surface (creases, pleats, fly, pockets, a soft break over the shoe), solid leather belt with loops and a metal buckle, sneakers fitted to the feet, eyes with an iris aimed at the lens, necklace and bead bracelet traced from the photo. Subdivides (two levels on the head, arms, tee and trousers), packs UVs and bakes position / normal / visibility maps. |
| 5 | `texture.py` | Colors every texel (4096 px head and arms): the enhanced photo wherever its camera saw that surface clearly; synthesized skin, stubble, buzz-cut hair and fabric elsewhere, tone-matched to the photo; stitch lines on the trousers. |
| 6 | `export.py` (+ `detail.py`) | PBR materials and glTF export: tiled detail normals (skin pores, jersey knit, chino twill, leather grain), cotton sheen, glossy eyes, metal chain and buckle. Desktop and mobile variants, plus a preview sheet. |
| 7 | `optimize.mjs` | Meshopt compression into `work/models/` (fine quantization for close-ups); the mobile file is simplified to ~40% of the triangles by meshopt's simplifier. |
| 8 | `lowpoly.py` | **The page's model.** Low-poly character measured off the guide (steps 1 to 4 with `--guide`); steps 1b and 5 to 7 are only for `./build.sh realistic`. |

`viewer.html` + `stations.mjs` render close-ups of every page stop (face, chest, hands, belt,
knees, feet) with three.js, the same renderer as the page: `npm run serve` in `work/tools`, then
`node stations.mjs` here → `build/preview/stations/`.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd work/tools/model
./setup.sh          # once: Python venv (Blender 5.2 as a module, MediaPipe, onnxruntime, numpy, scipy, pillow)
./build.sh          # full rebuild: ~25 minutes on 4 CPU cores (the photo upscale is cached after the first run)
./build.sh texture  # only re-texture + export (after texture.py tweaks)
```

`build/preview/final_sheet.png` shows the result; `build/preview/pose_v1_overlay.png` overlays the
posed model on the photo; `build/preview/enhance_face.png` compares the photo before and after
enhancement.

## Turning notes into changes

| Note | Where |
|---|---|
| Build: broader shoulders, bigger arms, slimmer waist, height | `character.json` → `macros`, `body` (MakeHuman targets, 0..1) |
| Face shape closer to / further from the photo | `character.json` → `face.regularization` (lower = follows the photo more), `face.residual` |
| Smile, squint, brows | `character.json` → `face.expression_units` (add / remove), or `regularization` |
| Face looks too smooth / too "retouched" | `character.json` → `texture.face_restore` (lower), `texture.photo_grain` (higher) |
| Eye color | `character.json` → `eyes.iris` (hex; default measured from the photo) |
| Arm pose | `character.json` → `pose` (wrist / elbow photo positions, depth, finger curl) |
| Tee: fit, sleeve length, collar | `character.json` → `outfit.tee` |
| Trousers: fit, leg width, length, belt height | `character.json` → `outfit.pants`, `outfit.belt` |
| Trousers: crease, pleat depth, break | `character.json` → `outfit.pants.tailor` (`crease`, `pleat`, `break`, meters) |
| Sneakers: colors, size, sole height | `character.json` → `outfit.sneakers` |
| Necklace path, clasp, bracelet position, bead size | `character.json` → `accessories` (photo pixels) |
| Colors of tee / trousers (default: measured from the photo) | `outfit.tee.color`, `outfit.pants.color` (hex) |
| Hairline, stubble, skin tone matching | `texture.py` (`synth_head`, `banded`) |
| Surface detail (pores, knit, twill, leather) | `detail.py` (patterns, `TILE_MM`), `export.py` → `LOOK` (strength, roughness, sheen) |

## Known limits

- From the front it is your photo; the sides and back of the head are educated guesses (the
  photo only shows one side). A profile photo would improve them the most; a set of photos all
  around (or a free phone scan app) would allow a true scan-quality likeness.
- The photo's lighting is baked into the face, so the page's lights shade it a second time.
- Arms, hands and the mouth interior come from one view, so they look best from the front.
- The legs, trousers below the belt and the sneakers are synthesized (the photo stops at the hips).
